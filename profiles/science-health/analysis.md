# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the scientific and practical importance of a research or health item, and the strength of the evidence behind it.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Paradigm-shifting.** A practice-changing clinical finding or major discovery with strong evidence.
- **7-8: Significant.** A peer-reviewed finding with clear implications for health, science, or policy.
- **5-6: Useful.** An incremental result or small study that adds context.
- **3-4: Low value.** Weak evidence, tiny sample, or a finding with no clear consequence.
- **0-2: Noise.** Hype, unreplicated preprint with no data, or promotional health content.

# Evaluation guidance

Note whether the work is peer-reviewed, the sample size, and whether it is in humans, animals, or cells. Do not treat a press release as a finding. Use three to five topic tags.
