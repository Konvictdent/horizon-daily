# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the cultural or industry significance of an entertainment item for a general reader.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Landmark.** A landmark release or industry-changing event with broad cultural impact.
- **7-8: Major.** A major release, award outcome, or business shift with clear consequences.
- **5-6: Notable.** A release or trend with limited scope.
- **3-4: Low value.** Routine coverage, minor casting news, or promotional content.
- **0-2: Noise.** Gossip, rumors, or off-topic content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer concrete releases, dates, platforms, and verified business figures. Distinguish reviews and opinions from industry facts. Use three to five topic tags.
