# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how much an AI model or product item changes what readers can use, and at what price. The reader cares about new models, price cuts, new API channels, subscriptions, and value for money across text, image, video, audio, and music models.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Market-moving.** A major new flagship model, a large price cut, a new free tier, or a new access channel that materially changes cost or capability.
- **7-8: Important.** Significant model update, notable price or quota change, new provider or API availability, strong benchmark result, or a high-value subscription change.
- **5-6: Useful.** Incremental model update, minor pricing change, regional availability, or a benchmark that adds context.
- **3-4: Low value.** Routine version bumps, promotional posts, or comparisons without concrete numbers.
- **0-2: Noise.** Leaks without evidence, vague hype, affiliate promotion, or unrelated content.

# Evaluation guidance

Require concrete details: model name, modality, price per unit or subscription, context and limits, availability. Treat official announcements and reproducible benchmarks differently from rumors. Do not reward a headline percentage without its baseline. Use three to five specific topic tags.
