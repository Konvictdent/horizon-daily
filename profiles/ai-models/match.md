# AI models and products profile

Use this profile for news about AI models and how to buy or access them, across all modalities: language and reasoning, image, video, audio, music, speech, 3D and code models.

Typical items include new model or version launches, price changes and discounts, free tiers and subscription plans, new API providers or channels, context-window and rate-limit changes, benchmark results, open-weight releases, and fine-tuning or tooling that materially changes access or cost.

Do not use this profile for generic AI business or funding news without a product, price, or access change; academic papers without an available model; or coding tutorials.
