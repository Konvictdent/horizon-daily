# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the significance of international and social news for a general Chinese reader: how many people are affected, how severe and verifiable the development is, and whether it changes daily life, safety, or public understanding.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Major event.** Large-scale conflict, disaster, or development with broad human impact.
- **7-8: Significant.** A development in a country or region with clear consequences for many people.
- **5-6: Notable.** A social story with limited scope but real substance.
- **3-4: Low value.** Routine incident, local story with little wider relevance, or thin reporting.
- **0-2: Noise.** Sensationalism, unverified rumor, or off-topic content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer verified facts with named sources, dates, and locations. Treat casualty figures as provisional. Do not reward outrage or speculation. Use three to five topic tags.
