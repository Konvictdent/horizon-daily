# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how much a political or policy item changes rules, power, or public life, and for whom.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Systemic change.** Landmark legislation, a major election outcome, war or peace decisions, or regulation affecting entire industries or countries.
- **7-8: Consequential.** A decision with clear effects on a country, institution, or large group.
- **5-6: Notable.** A political development with limited immediate impact.
- **3-4: Low value.** Routine politics, horse-race coverage, or statements without consequences.
- **0-2: Noise.** Unverified rumor, partisan spin, or off-topic content.

# Evaluation guidance

Distinguish announced policy from enacted law and from proposals. Identify who is bound by the decision and when it takes effect. Use three to five topic tags.
